Why do some stats have a plus?

Each stat has a score (the number you type, usually 8 to 20) and a bonus (the small + or − number under it). Only the bonus gets added to your roll. A score of 10 or 11 is ordinary, so its bonus is +0. Every 2 points above 10 adds +1. Below 10, the bonus goes negative.

Score8–910–1112–1314–1516–1718–1920
Bonus−1+0+1+2+3+4+5

What adds up when you roll: the d20 + the stat's bonus + your proficiency (only if you tick Trained, meaning your hero is practiced at this kind of thing) + gear + any other bonus from items or companions. Meet or beat the DC and it's a hit.

Your scores grow as you level up, which is how your bonuses climb toward the big DCs later on.
